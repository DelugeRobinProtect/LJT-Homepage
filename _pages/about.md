## About

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. Junteng graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. The research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Current research interests include:

- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
